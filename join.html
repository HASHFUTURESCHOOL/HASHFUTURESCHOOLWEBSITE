This is not a job posting

Most forms ask where you studied. We ask what you did.

We are a school, but we do not run on credentials. We run on people who notice what is broken and cannot leave it alone. So the form below is deliberately different.

The usual application
  • Upload your CV
  • List your degrees and certificates
  • Name your previous employers
  • Wait to be filtered by software
  • Never hear back
Ours
  • Show us what you actually built or did
  • Tell us what changed because of you
  • Tell us what you would change in the world
  • Tell us what you would contribute to this ecosystem
  • A human reads it and replies

The heart of the application

Three questions. Answer them like a human.

Rough honesty beats polished emptiness. Nobody here was hired because their answer sounded impressive — they were invited because it sounded true.

01

What have you really done?

Not where you studied — what you did in your life so far. A venture you started. A tool you built. A person you changed. A thing you fixed, taught, organised or grew. Add as many as you like, with the year and a link if it exists.

For example: "At 17 I rebuilt my village library's catalogue and got 300 children reading again."
02

What would you change in the world?

Name the thing that genuinely bothers you — about learning, about opportunity, about how children are treated by systems. Then tell us what you would change if you had the opportunity to.

For example: "I want a world where a child's curiosity is not punished by a timetable."
03

What will you contribute to this ecosystem?

This is the one that matters most. Our ecosystem is children, parents, facilitators and builders. What do you bring that is not already here — a skill, a perspective, a network, a habit, a kind of energy?

For example: "I can turn our sessions into short films that make parents trust us before they ever meet us."

What happens after you apply

No black hole. You will hear from a person.

1

You submit

Your answers are stored immediately, the team is emailed, and you get a reference number.

2

We read it — properly

Every application is read by a person, usually within a week. We read your achievements before anything else.

3

A real conversation

If there is a fit, we invite you to a 20–30 minute conversation about what you want to change.

4

We build something small together

A short real task — so you can see how we work, and we can see how you think.

5

You join the ecosystem

Or we stay in touch. Even when the timing is wrong, good minds are worth keeping close.
