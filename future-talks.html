About Future Talks

The stage that childhood built — and business earned.

Most summits wait until founders are grown-ups. Future Talks doesn't. It is India's first large-scale entrepreneurship summit built entirely for junior founders under 17 — kids and teens who started a venture while still in school, homeschool, or even kindergarten.

Think of it as TechSparks for the school generation: two days of fireside chats, live startup pitches, investor conversations, masterclasses and a demo day — where the youngest minds in India teach us what "starting early" really means.

✓ Only founders aged 7 – 17 on stage
✓ Real businesses, real revenue stories
✓ Parents welcomed as co-founders in life
✓ Investor & mentor feedback zones

"In traditional school, you learn line by line to write an exam and forget it the next day. Here, we build real projects, real products and real businesses."

— Shreya Soorej, 13 · Co-founder of a concept bakery
