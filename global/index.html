The model

One programme, three layers

Every pathway runs on the same operating system: a short intensive to re-engage, small-group mastery work to stabilise, and board preparation to certify.

SuperKids

40-day, 80-hour intensive AI mastery workshop for ages 8-17: 20+ generative AI tools, web apps, public speaking and entrepreneurship.

SuperLearn

Small-group mastery track for learners who need targeted academic support alongside project work.

Open Schooling Pathways

Guided preparation toward recognised external boards - NIOS, Cambridge IGCSE as a private candidate, and GED.

Global pods and mentors

Small international cohorts, house teams, student-led committees and a 1:8 mentor ratio, running live across time zones.
