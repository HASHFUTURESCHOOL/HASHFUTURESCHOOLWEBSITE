Homeschooling & boards

Homeschooling support in Ernakulam and across Kerala

Families in Kochi and Ernakulam who homeschool usually hit the same three walls: no consistent structure, no peer group, and no credible answer to "how will my child get a certificate?" We remove all three at once.

Days that stay organised

Your child joins live sessions with mentors, works on projects with a cohort, and reviews progress every week. The timetable does the nagging so your relationship with your child doesn't have to.

A certificate path you can plan around

We guide students toward three routes and help you choose based on age, goals and the subjects your child actually needs:

  • NIOS — the National Institute of Open Schooling, an Indian open board under the Ministry of Education. Flexible subject combinations and exam centres across Kerala. See the NIOS pathway →
  • IGCSE — Cambridge's international curriculum taken as a private candidate, popular with families planning to study or work abroad. See the IGCSE pathway →
  • GED — an internationally recognised high-school equivalency route for older students who want a faster, test-based credential.
A note on honesty: Hash Future School prepares and guides learners for these external boards. We are not an NIOS-accredited institution and we are not a Cambridge-registered school. Your child appears for the official examination through the board's own centres, and we make sure they arrive prepared, registered and on schedule.
