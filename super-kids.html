FAQ

Questions Parents Ask

What age group is this for? +
Super Kids is designed for school-going students aged 8 to 17. The content and projects are adapted to suit different age levels within this range.
Can my child do this alongside regular school? +
Absolutely! Sessions run from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM IST on weekdays, so they don't conflict with school hours. Weekend batches are also available.
What if my child misses a session? +
All sessions are recorded and available for students to review at their convenience. So if your child misses a class, they can catch up anytime.
What technology/device is needed? +
A laptop or desktop with a stable internet connection is all your child needs. Sessions are conducted on Microsoft Teams. No special software purchases are required — all tools are provided.
What's the difference between MINI, MAX, and PRO? +
MINI (10 days) covers foundational skills like presentation, research, and design. MAX (25 days) adds animations, video editing, basic AI tools, and YouTube. PRO (40 days) includes everything plus advanced AI tools, coding, website development, and a business pitch project. Each tier builds on the previous one.
Does my child get a certificate? +
Yes! Every student receives a recognized certificate upon completing their chosen program. This can be added to their portfolio and showcased to schools or future opportunities.
Is there a free demo or trial session? +
We'd be happy to walk you through the program in detail. Reach out to us on WhatsApp at +91 94971 20591 or click the "Book Now" button to connect with our team.
When does the next batch start? +
New batches start regularly. With only 10 seats per batch, we recommend enrolling early. Contact us on WhatsApp to know the next available start date.
