Rules & Guidelines

Fair play, real work

Seven simple rules keep the challenge fun, honest and globally fair.

1
Team & Age LimitTeams of 1–5 founders, each aged 7–17 at the time of registration. International teams are welcome.
2
Adult MentorEvery team needs one adult mentor (parent, guardian or teacher) who acts as the point of contact.
3
Parental ConsentParent/guardian consent is mandatory for every participant before the challenge begins.
4
Original VenturesAll ventures must be original and built during the challenge. Existing businesses may enter with a fresh expansion project.
5
Submission TemplatesPhase submissions follow the provided templates and deadlines. Late submissions lose milestone points.
6
Honest Use of AIAI tools are encouraged as assistants, but the ideas, decisions and work must be the team's own.
7
Certification EligibilityCertificates are awarded to every team that completes all three phases within the deadlines.
8
Code of ConductRespectful, kind and honest behaviour — online and at the finale. The challenge is about building, not tearing down.
